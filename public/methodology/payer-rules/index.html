Our evaluation method

A requirement needs a source and a scope.

This is the standard used to structure a claims-workflow evaluation. Confirm operational adherence and supported coverage before a pilot.

Source

Start with the current primary payer/program instructions. Retain the exact section and retrieval date.

Applicability

Identify payer, program, jurisdiction, service, claim format and effective period.

Review

Have a qualified reviewer explain the issue, uncertainty and appropriate next action.

Measure the whole review.

Useful findings matter, but so do duplicate alerts, false alerts and unavailable information. Compare the additional work with what the existing stack already does.

Record rule versions, changes and review decisions. If guidance conflicts or its effective date is unclear, mark it for investigation instead of silently treating it as current.

Questions worth answering

Does a payer guide establish product support?

No. Educational source coverage and a verified product data route are different things.

Does a passed check guarantee payment?

No. A scoped check addresses a specific requirement; payer adjudication and payment depend on additional factors.
