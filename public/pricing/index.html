A few useful answers

Before you take the next step.

Is there a standard rate on this website?

Pricing is quoted for the agreed scope. Contact sales@resolta.ai to discuss your workflow and request written terms.

What should we measure during an evaluation?

Use comparable claims and time periods. Track preparation and correction time, useful findings, duplicate checks and false alerts. Include any new software or service costs.

Is the workflow planner free to use?

Yes. The public planner, checklist, status guide and calculator are available without an account.
