Keep your team focused on the claims that need attention.

For agencies with recurring supported public-payer claims and a billing team that needs clearer preparation, checks and follow-up.

A practical starting point.

Begin with one payer/program that accounts for meaningful recurring work. Map the source documents, claim preparation, current checks and responses before choosing a tool.

Authorization and EVV records may be evidence inputs where supported. Resolta’s focus is the claim and reimbursement workflow.

Good fit to evaluate

  • Recurring claims and correction work
  • A billing owner who can assess findings
  • A specific payer/program and data route
  • Willingness to compare with the current process

Questions worth answering

Which payers are supported?

Confirm the exact payer, program, jurisdiction, claim format and data route during a workflow review. A payer guide is not a statement of product support.

Does Resolta replace our agency software?

The review focuses on claims and reimbursement work alongside the current process. Scheduling, caregiver management and EVV capture are outside this commercial scope.

Start with one claims workflow.

Agree a payer, a recent period and a small sample. Find out where an additional review is useful — including when it is not.
