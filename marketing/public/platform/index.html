A few useful answers

Before you take the next step.

Why add this if our clearinghouse checks claims?

Compare an additional review with the checks you already have. Useful findings, duplicate alerts, false alerts and missing information all belong in that comparison.

Does passing a check guarantee payment?

No. A scoped check addresses a particular requirement. Payer adjudication and payment depend on additional factors.

Can we see our workflow demonstrated?

Email sales@resolta.ai with your payer and process. Confirm the supported route and an appropriate demonstration with the team. Do not send patient or claim details by email.
