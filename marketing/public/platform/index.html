Illustrative walkthrough, not a live product demo or customer claim.

1 · Start with evidence

Identify the referral or authorization and invoice information used to prepare a draft. Missing information stays visible.

2 · Explain the check

Record the payer/program, source requirement and why it applies. A populated field alone does not prove the evidence is correct.

3 · Preserve the decision

The billing reviewer decides whether a correction is needed and retains the reason. Useful and false alerts are both measured.

A response is evidence, not a shortcut to “paid.”

Identify who sent each acknowledgment and what it says. Separate payer acceptance from adjudication, remittance and cash received.

Questions worth answering

Why add this if our clearinghouse checks claims?

Only add another tool if a scoped comparison finds meaningful additional value. The review records what the current stack already catches and what remains useful, duplicate, false or unavailable.

Does a risk score predict whether a payer will pay?

No predictive accuracy is asserted here. Review the underlying applicable checks and actual payer responses.
