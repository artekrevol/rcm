Home care claim checklist

A general evidence worksheet; payer-specific rules require applicable current sources.

Use this worksheet for a bounded home-care claims review. It is a general evidence checklist, not a payer rulebook or a statement of Resolta coverage. Complete the applicability block before using it. Patient or claim-level data belongs only in your approved secure system.
