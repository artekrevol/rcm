Home care claim exception taxonomy

A practical classification, not measured customer error rates.

A proposed classification for consistent reviews. These are not measured rates or findings from customer claims. Severity and business impact require a human decision; missing evidence must not be mislabeled a payer error.

IDCategoryDefinitionAction
E01Missing source evidenceRequired record unavailableUnavailable, not automatically an error
E02Identifier mismatchConflicting member/provider/claim valuesVerify authoritative source
E03Authorization scope mismatchService/date/unit inconsistency with applicable evidenceBilling review; no agency operations claim
E04Structural claim issueRequired structure, type or field absentValidate against format and route
E05Payer requirement issueDocumented applicable rule mismatchShow source and human correction
E06Submission or routing issueNo transmission evidence or wrong confirmed routeCheck transport and enrollment
E07Response interpretation issueSender or status not understoodInspect original response
E08Reconciliation exceptionClaim/line/remittance/deposit cannot be linkedKeep unresolved until evidence exists
E09Follow-up or deadline uncertaintyApplicable deadline or owner absentVerify payer rule and escalate
E10False or redundant alertReview finds no actionable incremental issueLog reviewer reason and revise rule

Required record: exception_id; de-identified claim reference; taxonomy_id; payer/program; applicable rule and version; available inputs; alert evidence; reviewer; useful/duplicate/false/unavailable decision; action; response; outcome; permission state. Prioritize submission-blocking verified errors first, uncertainty next for investigation, and informational notes last.
