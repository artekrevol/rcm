RESOLTA — GENERAL HOME CARE CLAIM CHECKLIST
Version 1.0. Generic worksheet; payer module requires review. Do not enter patient information in the GTM folder.

Scope: payer/program ___ jurisdiction ___ service period ___ format ___ route ___ reviewer ___

[ ] Scope
Identify payer, program, jurisdiction, service dates, claim format and submission route.
Evidence: Current payer/enrollment records
Exception: Unverified applicability

[ ] Source documents
Identify referral or authorization, invoice and relevant service evidence. Use approved secure storage.
Evidence: Record reference, owner and availability
Exception: Missing evidence

[ ] Identity
Check patient/member and submitting provider identifiers against the applicable source.
Evidence: Approved source and matching fields
Exception: Identifier mismatch

[ ] Authorization evidence
Compare applicable services, dates and units to the received authorization; do not infer authorization from a filled field.
Evidence: Authorization record and applicable payer rule
Exception: Scope mismatch or unavailable

[ ] Claim fields
Check required fields, dates, units, modifiers and format only against applicable rules.
Evidence: Rule ID and source version
Exception: Field/rule issue

[ ] Payer specifics
Confirm exactly which payer requirement applies; distinguish structural checks from payer rules.
Evidence: Program, effective range, source section, reviewer
Exception: Rule applicability uncertain

[ ] Human review
Confirm corrections and record unresolved exceptions before submission.
Evidence: Reviewer, action and timestamp
Exception: Unresolved exception

[ ] Submission
Retain the actual transmission evidence and correlation ID. Generating a file is not transmission.
Evidence: Transport response and sender
Exception: No transmission evidence

[ ] Response
Identify the sender and meaning of each acknowledgment; retain rejection/correction branch.
Evidence: Original acknowledgment and claim match
Exception: Status unclear

[ ] Follow-up
Record action owner and payer-specific due-date source; do not insert a universal deadline.
Evidence: Applicable filing/follow-up rule
Exception: Deadline not verified

[ ] Remittance
Match claim/line response, adjustments and remittance separately from bank receipt.
Evidence: ERA/EOB and reconciliation record
Exception: Unmatched or unresolved balance

[ ] Closeout
Document final state and evidence; do not equate absence of response with denial or loss.
Evidence: Outcome evidence and reviewer
Exception: Insufficient outcome evidence
