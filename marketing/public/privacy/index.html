Public tool privacy

Useful tools.
No claim data required.

This page explains the current public website tools and how to contact Resolta about them.

Planner, checklist and calculator

Tool entries are processed in your browser page. These tools do not submit the entries to Resolta, save them to browser storage or upload claim files. Reloading the page clears the entries. A download saves a copy on your device.

Choosing to email

Email buttons open your configured email application. You review and send the message yourself. If you email a workflow plan, the selected process answers appear in that draft. Do not include patient names, member identifiers, claim files or other sensitive details.

Website requests

Loading this website makes ordinary network requests to our hosting service. Hosting infrastructure may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and requested pages. The public tools do not load advertising trackers or send their input values to an analytics service.

The application is separate

The authenticated Resolta application has a different purpose and handles information according to the applicable customer arrangements. This page describes the public website tools, not the application’s contractual data terms.

Questions

Contact sales@resolta.ai for the appropriate privacy or customer-data contact before sharing sensitive information.
