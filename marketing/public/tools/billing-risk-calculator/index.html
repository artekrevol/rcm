The math is visible.

Preparation hours

Claims × preparation minutes ÷ 60

Correction hours

Corrected claims × total correction minutes ÷ 60

Monthly labor cost

Total hours × loaded hourly cost

Net monthly difference

Baseline labor − comparison labor − additional fees − allocated implementation cost

A negative comparison means the alternative costs more. Use comparable work and account for sampling differences before making a decision.
