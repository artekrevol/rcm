Claim preparation and correction cost calculator

Transparent labor estimates using your own inputs. No assumed savings.

Estimate monthly labor cost using your own observations. This tool estimates work, not recoverable revenue or promised savings. Inputs remain in this browser; no values are transmitted.

Transparent formulas

Preparation hours = claims × preparation minutes ÷ 60. Correction hours = affected claims × total correction minutes ÷ 60. Labor cost = (preparation hours + correction hours) × loaded hourly cost. Count correction time separately from original preparation; include repeated correction attempts in total correction minutes. Corrected claims may come from an older cohort, so they are not constrained to current-month prepared claims.

To compare a pilot, calculate both periods with equivalent scope and observed timings. Net labor benefit = baseline labor cost − pilot labor cost − incremental software/service fees − allocated implementation cost. Show a negative result when costs increase. Do not add claim balances, accelerated cash and recovered cash together. A delay is not necessarily a loss.
