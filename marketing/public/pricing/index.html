Pricing

Agree scope and pricing before a pilot begins.

A useful quote starts with a defined payer workflow, data route and responsibilities.

What the quote should make clear.

Workflow scope

Payer/program, claim format, expected volume and supported actions.

Implementation

Data preparation, enrollment dependencies, onboarding and human review.

Commercial terms

Pilot fees, recurring fees, usage limits, support, term and exit conditions.

Put a value on the work.

Use observed labor and correction time to compare costs. Do not assume every unresolved balance is recoverable revenue.

Try the labor-cost calculator →

Questions worth answering

Is there a published standard rate?

This launch foundation does not publish a rate. Pricing must be agreed in writing for the proposed scope.

Is a workflow review a guarantee of savings?

No. It can identify useful work, missing evidence or no meaningful additional value.

Start with one claims workflow.

Agree a payer, a recent period and a small sample. Find out where an additional review is useful — including when it is not.
